AI-generated art has changed illustration, and as a working illustrator I'm living with both the good and the bad of it.

In 2024, I lost two clients to image generators. It was a professional setback, and it felt personal too.

I still use AI tools for reference gathering, and they speed up my workflow. So I resist them and use them at the same time.

The training-data consent problem is still unresolved, and it hangs over the industry.

Artists, technologists, and policymakers will need to work out rules that are fair to the people whose work trained these tools.
