# My sourdough journey: lessons learned

I started baking sourdough in March 2023 with no idea what I was getting into. My first starter died after about two weeks, which was discouraging. It taught me that a starter is a living culture and needs regular feeding, not occasional attention.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour changed things. The starter started bubbling and stayed active.

Now I bake twice a week, and my dough runs at 78% hydration. I still have bad loaves sometimes, but I stopped letting them bother me.

If you're thinking about starting, expect to lose a starter or two. Mine was the first of several lessons, and I'm glad I kept going.
