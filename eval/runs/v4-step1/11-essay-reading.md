Reading is more than a hobby for me. It shows me other worlds and ties me to ideas from across time and space.

Last year I read 52 books and tracked every one in a carefully organized spreadsheet: title, author, date, rating. It was efficient and satisfying, and it felt hollow.

This year I stopped tracking. I've read 19 books so far, and I'm enjoying every page more than before. Without the pressure of numbers, reading feels less like a race to a total.

That doesn't make tracking bad, but the metrics we use can reshape the experiences we value. When we measure something, we change it.

I'll admit I still miss the spreadsheet a little. There's something comforting about seeing progress laid out in neat rows.

What stays with me are the stories, long after the final page.
