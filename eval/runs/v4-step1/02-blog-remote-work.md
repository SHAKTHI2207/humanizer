# What we learned from going remote

Our team of 12 went fully remote in 2021. It changed how we communicate, how we make decisions, and how we stay connected.

We moved standups from daily to three times a week, which gave people more time to focus while keeping everyone aligned. Any decision over $5,000 now needs a written proposal, so the reasoning is visible to everyone and someone is accountable for it. Twice a year we meet in person for an offsite.

It hasn't worked for everyone. One engineer left because of isolation, a reminder that remote work doesn't suit every person. For the rest of us, how we work has mattered more than where.
