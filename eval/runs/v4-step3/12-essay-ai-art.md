AI-generated art has changed illustration more than anything else in my working life. I'm a working illustrator, and I don't have a tidy position on it.

In 2024, I lost two clients to image generators. That was a professional setback, and it also made me ask what human creativity is worth now.

I still use AI tools myself, for gathering reference, because they speed up my workflow. So I resist the technology and use it in the same week, and I haven't resolved that.

What bothers me most is consent. The question of whether training data was used with permission is still open, and it hangs over the whole industry.

Artists, technologists, and policymakers need to work together on rules that protect creative work and are fair to the people whose work trained these tools.
