# What we learned going remote

Our team of 12 went fully remote in 2021. It changed how we communicate, make decisions, and spend time together.

We moved standups from daily to three times a week, which gave people more time to focus while keeping us in sync. Any decision over $5,000 now needs a written proposal, so everyone can see the reasoning and who made the call. Twice a year we meet in person for an offsite.

It hasn't all gone well. One engineer left because of isolation, a reminder that remote work doesn't suit everyone.

For us, though, it has worked. What matters is how the team works, and where people sit has turned out to matter much less.
