Reading is a hobby, but it also feels like a way into other worlds and a way of seeing my own more clearly.

Last year I read 52 books and tracked every one in a spreadsheet: title, author, date, rating. It was efficient and satisfying, and somehow hollow.

This year I stopped tracking. I've read 19 books so far, and I'm enjoying every page more than I did last year. With no numbers to chase, reading feels less like a goal to hit.

That doesn't make tracking bad. But the metrics we use can reshape the experiences we value, and measuring something changes it.

I'll admit I still miss the spreadsheet a little. There's something comforting about seeing progress laid out in neat rows.

What I remember, though, is the stories that stay with me long after the final page.
