Few urban planning policies have done as much damage with as little notice as parking minimums. Most people have never heard of them, yet they shape what gets built in our cities.

Parking minimums affect housing, affordability, and the kind of neighborhoods we end up with. When a city requires developers to include parking, new housing costs more to build. That prices out the very residents cities say they want to serve.

Take Minneapolis. In 2021 the city eliminated parking minimums citywide. I think that was the right call, and it shows what a city can do when it puts people ahead of pavement.

Some neighborhoods do have real parking shortages, and that deserves a fair hearing. But the question is not whether we need parking. It is who should decide how much, and I don't think a blanket requirement should.

Cities should drop these rules.
