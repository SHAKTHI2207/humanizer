# My sourdough journey

I started baking sourdough in March 2023, and it has taught me patience whether I wanted the lesson or not.

My first starter died after about two weeks. That was a bad moment, but it showed me that a starter is a living culture that needs regular attention and care.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour fixed it. The starter got bubbly and active almost right away.

Today I bake twice a week, and I'm happy with my 78% hydration dough. It took a while to get here.

If you're starting out, trust the process, expect some failures, and enjoy the small wins. Happy baking! 🍞
