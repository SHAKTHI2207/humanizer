# Ledgerly 2.0 is here

Ledgerly 2.0 is available starting June 3. It's an update for freelancers who manage their own invoicing.

Two things are new:

- Recurring invoices. Set one up and Ledgerly sends it on schedule.
- Multi-currency support. You can bill clients in 24 currencies.

Pricing stays at $9 per month, with no hidden fees.
