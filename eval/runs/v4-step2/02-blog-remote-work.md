# What we learned going remote

Our team of 12 went fully remote in 2021, and the change touched how we communicate, how we decide things, and how we stay connected.

We moved standups from daily to three times a week, which gave people more time to focus while keeping everyone aligned. For any decision over $5,000, we now require a written proposal, so the reasoning is on record and someone is accountable for it. Twice a year we meet in person for an offsite.

It hasn't worked for everyone. One engineer left, citing isolation, and that was a reminder that remote work doesn't suit every person.

For us, though, how we work has mattered more than where we work.
