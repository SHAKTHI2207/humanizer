# My sourdough journey

I started baking sourdough in March 2023, and I had no idea what I was getting into.

My first starter died after about two weeks. That was a rough start, but it taught me that a starter is a living thing and needs regular feeding and attention.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour made the difference. My starter finally started bubbling and stayed active.

Today I bake twice a week, and my 78% hydration dough is something I'm proud of. If you're just starting out, expect some failures, trust the process, and enjoy the small wins. Happy baking!
