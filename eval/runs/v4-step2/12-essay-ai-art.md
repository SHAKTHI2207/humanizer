AI-generated art has changed illustration faster than anything else I've seen in my career, and as a working illustrator I'm of two minds about it.

In 2024 I lost two clients to image generators. That hurt professionally, and it also made me question what human-made work is worth to the people who hire for it.

I still use AI tools myself, for gathering reference, because they speed up my workflow. So I resist the technology and use it at the same time, and I haven't worked out how to square that.

What bothers me most is consent. The question of whether training data was used with permission is still unresolved, and it hangs over the whole industry.

Artists, technologists, and policymakers need to work this out together, and fairness has to be part of the answer.
