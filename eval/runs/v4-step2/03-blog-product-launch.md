# Introducing Ledgerly 2.0

Ledgerly 2.0 is available starting June 3. It's an update for freelancers who manage their own invoicing.

Two things are new. Recurring invoices are handled by Ledgerly, so you don't have to recreate them each billing cycle. And you can now bill clients in 24 currencies.

Pricing stays at $9 per month, with no hidden fees.
