Last year I read 52 books and tracked every one in a spreadsheet: title, author, date, rating. It was efficient and satisfying, and after a while it felt hollow.

This year I stopped tracking. I've read 19 books so far, and I'm enjoying them more than I did last year. Without a number to hit, I read at whatever pace the book wants.

That doesn't make tracking bad. But the metrics we pick can quietly change the thing we're measuring, and I think that happened to my reading.

I'll admit I still miss the spreadsheet a little. Seeing progress laid out in neat rows was comforting.
