# What sourdough has taught me so far

Baking sourdough has been a lesson in patience. When I started in March 2023, I had no idea what I was in for.

My first starter died after about two weeks, and I took it harder than I'd like to admit. It did teach me that a starter is a living culture, and it needs steady attention.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour changed everything. Almost overnight, the starter was healthy and bubbling.

Now I bake twice a week, and my 78% hydration dough shows me how far I've come.
