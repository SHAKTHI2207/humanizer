# What we learned from going remote

When our team of 12 went fully remote in 2021, it changed our culture, our processes, and how we collaborate.

We cut standups from daily to three times a week, which gave people more time to focus while keeping everyone in sync. Any decision over $5,000 now needs a written proposal, so the reasoning is out in the open and someone owns it. And twice a year we meet in person for an offsite to keep the team close.

It wasn't all smooth. One engineer left, citing isolation, which reminded us that remote work doesn't suit everyone.
