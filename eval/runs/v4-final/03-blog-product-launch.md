# Ledgerly 2.0: invoicing, reimagined

Ledgerly 2.0 is available June 3, with changes to how freelancers manage their finances.

Freelancers have a lot to juggle, so we built this release to make the business side simpler. It adds two things.

Recurring invoices: set one up once and Ledgerly handles the rest.

Multi-currency support: bill clients in 24 currencies, so you can work with clients around the world.

The price stays at $9 a month, with no hidden fees.
