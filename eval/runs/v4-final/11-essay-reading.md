Last year I read 52 books and logged every one in a carefully organized spreadsheet: title, author, date, rating. It was efficient and satisfying, and it felt hollow.

This year I stopped tracking. I've read 19 books so far, and I'm enjoying every page more than I ever have. Without a number to hit, reading feels less like heading for a finish line.

That doesn't make tracking bad. The metrics we use can subtly reshape the experiences we value, because measuring something changes it.

Still, I'll admit I miss the spreadsheet a little. There's something comforting about seeing progress laid out in neat rows.
