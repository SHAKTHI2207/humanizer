## Limitations and future directions

This study has several limitations. All participants came from a single university, so the findings may not generalize to other institutions, although similar patterns may hold elsewhere in higher education. Workload was self-reported through a weekly survey, which leaves room for reporting bias; the responses were consistent, which offers some reassurance about their reliability. The design was also cross-sectional, so it cannot establish cause.

Workload was associated with burnout (beta = 0.12). This fits the view that workload management matters and that institutions may need to intervene, but the cross-sectional design means the association does not show that reducing workload would reduce burnout. A longitudinal follow-up could show how burnout develops over time and help identify interventions that work.
