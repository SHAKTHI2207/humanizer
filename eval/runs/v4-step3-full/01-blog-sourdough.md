# My sourdough journey: lessons learned along the way

I started baking sourdough in March 2023, and it has mostly taught me patience.

My first starter died after about two weeks. That was devastating. But it showed me that a starter is a living culture that needs regular attention, not just flour and water.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour changed things. My starter began to bubble and stayed active.

Now I bake twice a week, and my 78% hydration dough shows how far I've come.

Trust the process, expect some failures, and enjoy the small wins. Happy baking!

# My sourdough journey: lessons learned along the way

I started baking sourdough in March 2023, and mostly what it has taught me is patience.

My first starter died after about two weeks. That was devastating. But it taught me that a starter is a living culture that needs regular attention, not just flour and water.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour changed things. The starter began to bubble and stayed active.

These days I bake twice a week, and my 78% hydration dough is a decent measure of how far I've come.

Trust the process, expect some failures, and enjoy the small wins. Happy baking!
