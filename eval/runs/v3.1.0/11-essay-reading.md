Reading opens onto other worlds and shows me my own, and it connects me to ideas from other times and places.

Last year I read 52 books and tracked each one in a spreadsheet: title, author, date, rating. It was efficient and satisfying, and it felt hollow.

This year I stopped tracking. I've read 19 books so far, and I'm enjoying every page more than ever. Without the pressure of numbers, I'm no longer racing toward a count.

Tracking isn't bad, though. The metrics we use can reshape the experiences we value, because when we measure something, we change it.

I'll admit I still miss the spreadsheet a little. Seeing progress laid out in neat rows is comforting.

What I keep from a book is the story that stays with me long after the last page.
