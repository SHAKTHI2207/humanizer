## Limitations and future directions

This study has several limitations. The sample came from a single university, so the findings may not generalize, although the patterns probably reflect broader trends in higher education. Workload was self-reported in a weekly survey, which leaves room for reporting bias, though the responses were consistent enough to suggest they are reliable. The design was cross-sectional, so it cannot establish causation.

Even so, the association between workload and burnout (beta = 0.12) supports efforts to manage workload, and the findings point to a need for institutional interventions. A longitudinal follow-up would show how burnout develops over time and could guide more targeted and lasting solutions.
