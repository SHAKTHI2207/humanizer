# What we learned going remote

Our team of 12 went fully remote in 2021. It changed how we communicate, make decisions, and spend time together.

We moved standups from daily to three times a week, which gave people more time to focus and kept us in sync. Any decision over $5,000 now needs a written proposal, so everyone can see who decided what and why. Twice a year we meet in person for an offsite, which helps us stay close as a team.

It hasn't all gone smoothly. One engineer left and cited isolation, a reminder that remote work doesn't suit everyone.

For us, how we work has mattered more than where we work, and the changes above have paid off.
