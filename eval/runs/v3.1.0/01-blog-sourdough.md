# My sourdough journey

I started baking sourdough in March 2023 and had no idea what I was getting into. Mostly it has taught me patience.

My first starter died after about two weeks. It was disappointing, but it showed me that a starter is a living culture that needs regular feeding and attention.

Switching to a 50/50 blend of whole wheat and white flour made the biggest difference. The starter started bubbling and stayed active.

Today I bake twice a week, and my 78% hydration dough shows how far I've come.

If you're thinking about starting, try it. Expect a few failures along the way, and enjoy the small wins when they come. Happy baking!
